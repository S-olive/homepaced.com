Our Story

About Homepaced

Built on the belief that a beautifully designed home should be accessible — not a luxury reserved for the few.

The Origin

Why We Started

Homepaced was founded on a simple frustration: the gap between what professional interior designers charge and what most homeowners can afford is enormous — and the DIY alternative often leads to costly mistakes and spaces that never feel finished.

We built Homepaced to occupy that gap. Drawing on a background in professional project management, a genuine passion for Art Deco and timeless design, and a talent for discovering exceptional pre-owned pieces, we created a service that brings real design thinking to real budgets.

The name says it all: your home, designed at your pace, on your terms.

Our Approach

Slow Interior Design

"Slow Design" isn't about moving slowly — it's about moving intentionally. It means resisting the pull of trends, taking time to understand how a space actually functions and feels, and making decisions that will hold up for years, not just seasons. It means buying fewer pieces, but better ones. And it means that a home's character deepens over time, rather than dating itself the moment the next trend arrives.

What Drives Us

Our Values

Value 01

Accessibility

Exceptional design should not require an exceptional budget. We're committed to making professional-quality design planning and furniture sourcing available to people who have been priced out of traditional design services.

Value 02

Sustainability

The furniture industry is one of the most wasteful on the planet. By prioritizing the pre-owned market through our Reverse Consignment model, we actively reduce furniture waste and keep quality pieces in circulation — extending their life and their story.

Value 03

Intentionality

We don't believe in filling rooms. We believe in furnishing homes. Every piece recommended or sourced has a reason to be there — a functional purpose, an aesthetic contribution, and a quality standard that ensures it will last.

The Model

Reverse Consignment

Traditional consignment means selling your items through a middleman. We flip that model. Instead of sourcing on behalf of a seller, we source for the buyer — acting as your personal scout in the pre-owned market.

We identify high-quality, well-cared-for pieces, vet their condition, negotiate the price, and coordinate logistics — all on your behalf. You get the piece you want, without the time, effort, or frustration of navigating the secondhand market yourself.

The result: luxury-caliber furniture at resale prices, curated with a designer's eye.
